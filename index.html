Client Policies

Please review these policies before requesting an appointment.

First-Time Clients

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required upfront to secure your reservation and confirm your booking. Once the deposit is received, your booking code will be sent directly to you.

Guests & Companions

No additional guests, friends, or companions are permitted during meetups. Please arrive alone unless otherwise approved in advance.

Substances

If you are using or have used any drugs or other substances that could affect your behavior or safety, this must be disclosed beforehand.

Weapons Policy

Firearms and weapons of any kind are strictly prohibited. Anyone found carrying a weapon will not be permitted to proceed with the appointment.
